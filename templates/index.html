🧠 AI Behavior Analyzer

Welcome! This tool helps analyze your personality, emotional reactions, and decision-making style using AI. You'll be asked a series of structured questions across different aspects of your personality.

After answering all the questions, the AI will generate a behavioral analysis based on your responses.
You can also test how you might behave in a specific real-life situation by using the “Predict Behavior in Situation” option.

Furthermore: You can record your voice in responses instead of typing in each question box. The system automatically detects your language and writes the transcribed text in the question box.
